# CINCA - Ceramic Tile Catalogue

A full rebuild of CINCA's website as a product catalogue for architects, specifiers and residential buyers, covering the COLORTECH technical line and the residential collections.

## Scope of Work

- Web design and a custom WordPress theme built around the CINCA brand
- Spreadsheet-driven catalogue importer: 43 series and 4,300+ SKUs with sizes, finishes and photography
- Product pages with dynamic filters, metric and imperial units side by side
- 40+ custom Gutenberg blocks, so every section can be reused on any page
- Professionals area with registration, favourites, and a library of ~500 technical downloads
- Multilingual (PT, EN, FR) with translated URLs and legacy 301 redirects
- Reference projects, news, newsletter integration and GDPR cookie consent
- Performance hosting: FastCGI page cache, Redis object cache and WebP media
