# Godwit - Tour Booking for WooCommerce

A WordPress plugin for tour operators and travel agencies, built and maintained by NÍTIDA. Every trip gets a full trip page and sells through the operator's own WooCommerce store.

## Scope

- Trip pages with day-by-day itinerary, highlights, inclusions, gallery, map and FAQs
- Trip search with filters, a calendar and a favourites list
- Departures with their own date, price and seats, added to the WooCommerce cart
- Shared library of extras, mandatory or optional per trip
- Godwit Pro (godwitbooking.com): multiple departures, deposits and installments, passenger lists, reports
